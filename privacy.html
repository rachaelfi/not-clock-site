Not Clock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9/24/2026

The short version

  • Not Clock has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.
  • Your alarms, timers, world clocks, and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • One feature — World Clock — contacts a time service on the internet. That request includes your device's IP address. Nothing else leaves your device, ever.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize any information about you.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

The rest of this page explains the above in more detail.

1. Who this policy is from

Not Clock ("the app") is made by Rachael Park ("we", "us"). This policy describes what the app does with information. If anything here is unclear, email fisherra3119@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following to your device's private app storage so your setup survives closing and reopening the app:

  • Alarms you create — time, label, repeat days, chosen sound, and whether snooze and flash are on
  • Sleep alarm settings
  • Cities added to World Clock
  • Recently used timer durations
  • Preferences: 24-hour time, theme and accent colour, app icon, and the Night Clock toggle

This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us, and we have no way to see it. Other apps cannot read it. If your device has an encrypted backup enabled (iCloud Backup or Google's backup service), this data may be included in that backup — that is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us.

3. Information sent over the internet

The app makes network requests in exactly one situation: when you use the World Clock screen. To show accurate times and daylight saving status, it requests timezone information from time.now, a third-party time service.

Two kinds of request are made:

  • Looking up a city you added. The request contains the timezone name, such as Europe/Paris. It contains nothing about you.
  • Detecting your own timezone. This request has no parameters, but like any internet request it necessarily reveals your device's IP address to that service, which uses it to work out your approximate region.

An IP address is generally treated as personal information, which is why we call it out here rather than claiming the app sends nothing at all. We do not receive, log, or store your IP address — it goes to the time service, not to us. That service's handling of it is governed by its own privacy policy.

If you never open the World Clock screen, the app makes no network requests at all.

4. What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or profile
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No crash reporting services
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location services — the app never requests GPS access
  • No access to contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or health data
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of information with data brokers
  • No cookies or cross-app tracking

5. Purchases

If the app is offered for a price, or offers paid features, all payments are processed by Apple or Google through their own stores. They are the seller of record. We never see or handle your payment card, billing address, or any other payment details.

Apple and Google provide us with aggregate sales reports — numbers of downloads and revenue by country — which do not identify individual purchasers. Your purchase is also recorded against your Apple or Google account by them, so the app can be restored or reinstalled. That record is theirs, and is covered by their privacy policies.

6. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. We do not knowingly gather data from children, and since we receive no personal data at all, there is nothing for us to delete. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

7. Your rights over your data

Privacy laws including the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal data a company holds about you, and to opt out of its sale.

We hold no personal data about you. There is no database, no server, and no account. This means:

  • There is nothing for us to send you in response to an access request.
  • There is nothing for us to delete on our side. To erase everything the app saved, delete the app from your device — that removes all of it immediately and permanently.
  • There is no sale or sharing of personal information to opt out of, because none occurs.

For data held by Apple or Google about your download or purchase, contact them directly, as we cannot access or delete it.

8. Data retention

Data stored on your device is kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. We retain nothing, so there is no retention period on our side.

9. Security

App data is stored in your device's private application storage, which the operating system isolates from other apps and protects with the device's own encryption when a passcode or biometric lock is set. Network requests to the time service use HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure, but because the app holds nothing sensitive and transmits nothing about you, the consequences of any failure are limited to the alarm and clock settings on your own device.

10. Third-party services

The app relies on these third parties:

  • time.now — timezone and daylight saving data for the World Clock feature
  • Apple (iOS) and Google (Android) — app distribution, payment processing, and operating system services such as notifications and alarms

Each has its own privacy policy governing what it does with information it receives. We have no control over their practices.

11. International users

Because the app stores data only on your device, your information does not cross borders through us. Requests to the time service may be handled by servers in other countries.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example, if a future feature needed a network connection or a new permission — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
